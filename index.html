About This Project

The idea for this password generator website was born out of a need for better online security. I’ve always been passionate about finding practical solutions to everyday problems, and I noticed that many people still rely on weak or reused passwords, leaving their accounts vulnerable.

I wanted to create a tool that combines convenience, flexibility, and strong security features, making it easy for anyone to generate complex passwords without the hassle.

With this project, I aim to help users protect their online identities and secure their accounts with reliable, randomly generated passwords.
